Add `riyal/svelte` entry — first-class Svelte 5 support using runes (`$props`, `$state`, `$derived`, `$effect`, `$bindable`). Ships `RiyalSymbol`, `RiyalIcon`, `RiyalPrice`, `AnimatedRiyalPrice`, `RiyalInput`, and the `useRiyalRate` composable. The entry is shipped as `.svelte` source so consumers' Svelte tooling (Vite, SvelteKit) compiles it natively. Adds `svelte` (>=5) as an optional peer dependency, plus `svelte`, `svelte5`, and `sveltekit` keywords.
